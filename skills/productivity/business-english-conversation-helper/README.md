# Business English Skills

Reusable conversation-analysis and Business English reply skill.

Contents:
- `SKILL.md` — core behavior
- `templates/master-prompt.md` — reusable master instruction
- `templates/quick-conversation-reply.md` — full-chat template
- `templates/reply-only.md` — final-message-only template
- `tests/acceptance-tests.md` — verification scenarios

The skill analyzes conversation history supplied in context or retrieved through an authorized connected source. It does not automatically gain access to unavailable historical chats.
